# EMI dataset

**Consolidated_EMI_Dataset.xlsx** contains **1,748 shielding results from 284 articles**. Each row is one reported result. A sample may appear more than once when it has different test conditions or separate average and maximum results.

## Reading the columns

- **Matrix and filler:** material names, amounts and bases, such as wt% or vol%.
- **Thickness, frequency and Conditions:** the recorded sample and test details.
- **SE value and unit:** the reported shielding result.
- **SE metric:** the type of result, such as ordinary SE or normalized SE. 
- **SE value interpretation:** how the result was reported—for example, “about 20” or “at least 20.”

A **blank component slot** means no component is listed. **not specified** means information is unknown. **0** means a named material has a recorded loading of zero.
